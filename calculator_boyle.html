Boyle's Law: Pressure-Volume Relationship

Boyle's Law states that the pressure of a gas is inversely proportional to its volume when temperature and the number of moles are held constant. As pressure increases, volume decreases proportionally. This relationship was discovered by Robert Boyle in 1662.

P₁ × V₁ = P₂ × V₂

Where:

  • P₁ = Initial pressure
  • V₁ = Initial volume
  • P₂ = Final pressure
  • V₂ = Final volume

Boyle's Law is valid for ideal gases at constant temperature. It is used in applications like breathing (lungs), syringes, and pressure cookers.
