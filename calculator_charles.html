Charles's Law: Volume-Temperature Relationship

Charles's Law states that the volume of a gas is directly proportional to its absolute temperature when pressure and the number of moles are held constant. As temperature increases, volume increases proportionally. This relationship was discovered by Jacques Charles in 1787.

V₁ / T₁ = V₂ / T₂

Where:

  • V₁ = Initial volume
  • T₁ = Initial temperature (in Kelvin)
  • V₂ = Final volume
  • T₂ = Final temperature (in Kelvin)

Charles's Law explains why hot air balloons rise (hot air expands), and why balloons shrink in cold weather. Temperature must always be in Kelvin (K = °C + 273.15).
