About Steel Pipe Weight & Structural Design

Calculating the weight of steel pipes per meter and total length is crucial for structural design, lifting load assessments, transportation logistics, and pipeline suspension engineering.

Weight per Meter (kg/m) = 0.02466 · WT · (OD - WT)
Total Weight (kg) = Weight per Meter × Length (L)

Where:

  • OD = Outer diameter of the steel pipe in millimeters (mm)
  • WT = Wall thickness of the steel pipe in millimeters (mm)
  • 0.02466 = Standard carbon steel volumetric constant (derived from density ~7.85 $g/cm^3$)
  • L = Total pipe length in meters (m)

This standard industry formula simplifies structural calculations by factoring in the specific density of steel to output accurate mass per unit length instantly.
