🧪 Acid-Base Chemistry

pH, pOH & Acid-Base Equilibrium

The pH scale measures the acidity or basicity of an aqueous solution. It is defined as the negative logarithm of the hydrogen ion concentration. Solutions with pH < 7 are acidic, pH = 7 are neutral, and pH > 7 are basic (alkaline).

pH = -log₁₀[H⁺]  |  pOH = -log₁₀[OH⁻]  |  pH + pOH = 14

Key Relationships:

  • pH = Negative logarithm of hydrogen ion concentration
  • pOH = Negative logarithm of hydroxide ion concentration
  • pH + pOH = 14 at 25°C (water autoionization constant)
  • [H⁺] × [OH⁻] = 10⁻¹⁴ at 25°C (ion product of water)

pH is a crucial parameter in chemistry, biology, medicine, and environmental science. It affects enzyme activity, drug solubility, corrosion rates, and many industrial processes.
