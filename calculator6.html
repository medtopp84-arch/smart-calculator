About Hydraulic Flow Rate & Principles

Hydraulic flow rate calculations are crucial in fluid mechanics for designing water supply networks, open channels, drainage systems, and industrial piping.

Flow Rate (Q) = Area (A) × Velocity (V)
Flow (L/h) = Q (m³/s) × 1,000 × 3,600

Where:

  • Area (A) = Cross-sectional area of the flow path in square meters (m²)
  • Velocity (V) = Average fluid velocity in meters per second (m/s)
  • Flow Rate (Q) = Discharge volume per second in cubic meters (m³/s)
  • Flow (L/h) = Hourly volumetric flow rate converted to Liters per hour

Applying the continuity equation ensures proper pump sizing, pipe diameter selection, and prevents excessive frictional pressure losses in networks.
