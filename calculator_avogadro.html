Avogadro's Law: Volume-Moles Relationship

Avogadro's Law states that the volume of a gas is directly proportional to the number of moles of gas at constant temperature and pressure. Equal volumes of gases at the same temperature and pressure contain equal numbers of molecules.

V₁ / n₁ = V₂ / n₂

Where:

  • V₁, V₂ = Initial and final volume
  • n₁, n₂ = Initial and final number of moles

Avogadro's Law explains why balloon volume increases when more gas is added, and why 1 mole of any gas occupies 22.4 L at STP (Standard Temperature and Pressure).
