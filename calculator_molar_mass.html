Molar Mass Calculator

Molar mass is the mass of one mole of a substance (in g/mol). It is calculated by summing the atomic masses of all atoms in a chemical formula. This is essential for converting between mass and moles in chemical reactions.

Molar Mass = Σ (Atomic Mass × Number of Atoms)

Molar mass is used in stoichiometry to convert between grams and moles, and to determine the empirical and molecular formulas of compounds.
