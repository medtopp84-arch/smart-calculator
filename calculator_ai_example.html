About Solution Dilution & The C₁V₁ = C₂V₂ Principle

Solution dilution is the process of decreasing the concentration of a solute in a solution by adding more solvent. The dilution equation (C₁V₁ = C₂V₂) is one of the most fundamental formulas in chemistry and biochemistry for preparing working solutions from stock solutions.

C₁ × V₁ = C₂ × V₂

Where:

  • C₁ = Initial concentration of stock solution
  • V₁ = Volume of stock solution needed
  • C₂ = Final desired concentration
  • V₂ = Final volume of diluted solution

This equation is essential for preparing standard solutions, performing titrations, and creating serial dilutions in analytical chemistry and molecular biology.
