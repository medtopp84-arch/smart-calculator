Solution Dilution: C₁V₁ = C₂V₂

Dilution is the process of reducing the concentration of a solute in a solution by adding more solvent. The key principle is that the amount of solute remains constant during dilution.

C₁ × V₁ = C₂ × V₂

Where:

  • C₁ = Initial concentration
  • V₁ = Volume of stock solution
  • C₂ = Final concentration
  • V₂ = Final volume

The dilution equation is essential for preparing working solutions from stock solutions in chemistry labs, biochemistry, and medical applications.
