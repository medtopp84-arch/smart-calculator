About Concrete Volume & Weight Calculations

Accurate estimation of concrete volume is vital in structural engineering for ordering ready-mix trucks, managing budgets, and calculating total dead loads of foundations, slabs, and footings.

Volume (V) = Length × Width × Thickness
Weight (Tons) = (Volume × 2400 kg/m³) / 1000

Where:

  • Length (L) = Clear span length of the concrete element in meters
  • Width (W) = Clear span width of the concrete element in meters
  • Depth (D) = Thickness or depth of the slab/footing in meters
  • Density = Standard normal-weight reinforced concrete unit weight (~2400 kg/m³)

Always add a 5% to 10% wastage and spillage safety margin when ordering concrete truckloads to account for formwork deflection and uneven sub-grades.
