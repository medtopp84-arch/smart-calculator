About Pipeline Bends & Geometry

Pipeline bends (Cintre) calculations are critical in piping engineering and route design. They determine the physical arc length of the curved section and the tangent length required for fittings.

Arc Length = R · (Δ · π / 180)
Tangent Length = R · tan(Δ / 2)

Where:

  • R = Bend Radius of the pipeline centerline
  • Δ (Delta) = Deflection angle in degrees
  • Arc Length = Actual curved path distance of the pipe
  • Tangent Length = Distance from the Point of Intersection (PI) to the beginning/end of the curve

Piping engineers and layout designers use these geometrical relations to fabricate prefabricated spool pieces and align trench networks accurately.
