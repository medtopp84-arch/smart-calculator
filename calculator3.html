Advanced Pipeline Bends & Geometry Engineering

Pipeline bends (Cintre) calculations are critical in high-pressure piping engineering, petroleum transport networks, and routing layouts. They define exact arc lengths and tangent dimensions required for spool prefabrication and site fitting according to international codes (ASME B31.4 / B31.8).

Arc Length (L) = R · (Δ · π / 180)
Tangent Length (T) = R · tan(Δ / 2)

Engineering Parameters:

  • R = Centerline Bend Radius (Standard or Custom Code Radius)
  • Δ (Delta) = Deflection / Bending Angle in Degrees
  • Arc Length = Actual physical pipe length along the curvature
  • Tangent Length = Distance from the Intersection Point (PI) to the curve tangent point (TC/CT)

Utilized extensively by pipeline construction engineers to minimize fluid pressure drops and secure flawless alignment across complex geographical profiles.
