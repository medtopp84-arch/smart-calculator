Mass Percent Concentration

Mass percent (also called weight percent or w/w%) is a measure of concentration that expresses the mass of solute as a percentage of the total mass of the solution. It is commonly used in chemistry, pharmacy, and industrial applications.

Mass % = (mass of solute / mass of solution) × 100%

Where:

  • Mass of solute = Mass of the substance being dissolved
  • Mass of solution = Mass of solute + Mass of solvent

Mass percent is used in preparing solutions, quality control, and expressing the composition of mixtures and alloys.
